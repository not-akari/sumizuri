## A good bug report

Say which device and which version of Sumizuri you use, and what you did before it went wrong. A screenshot helps. The version shows on the **About** row in **Settings**.

## Rights holders

Sumizuri holds no content, so there is nothing in the app or on this website to take down. To have content removed, contact the site that hosts it. See the [terms](/terms).
