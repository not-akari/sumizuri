## What Sumizuri is

Sumizuri is a viewer. It shows the manga, comics, novels and anime you add yourself, and keeps your library, progress and settings.

It comes with no content, no sources and no repos. It shows nothing until you add something.

## What we do not provide

We do not host, upload, sell or hand out any content. We run no servers that hold content, and the app does not point you to any.

Sources and repos are made and shared by other people. We do not make, check, list or stand behind them, and we have no tie to the sites they reach.

A sync server is run by whoever hosts it. We do not run one for you.

## Your choices

Sumizuri does not limit what you add or open. What you add is your choice, and so is making sure you have the right to it where you live.

The sites a source reaches have their own terms. Those are between you and each site.

## Rights holders

Sumizuri holds no content, so there is nothing in the app or on this website to take down. To have content removed, contact the site that hosts it.

## No promise

Sumizuri is given as it is, with no promise that it works for any purpose. We are not responsible for what you do with it, or for what a source or a site does.

## This website

This website only describes the app and offers its downloads. The download links point to files on GitHub. The guides are written for people who make sources for sites and APIs they have the right to use.
