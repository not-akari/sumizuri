# Privacy

We collect nothing about you.

## In short

Sumizuri has no account with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, see or sell anything about you.

## Kept on your device

Your library, history, progress, profiles, settings, downloads, logs and the cookies of the sites you visit stay on your device.

Backups are files you make. They go where you save them, and you can lock one with a password.

## What leaves your device

- **Sources.** Sources you add ask the sites they reach straight from your device, as a browser would. Those sites see your IP address and can set cookies.
- **Repos.** Repos you add are read to install and update their sources.
- **Updates.** The app asks GitHub for new versions of Sumizuri. You can turn this off in App updates.
- **Word lookup.** A word you look up in the reader goes to Wiktionary or Jisho. Web search opens Google.
- **Translation.** When you turn on translation in the novel reader, the text of the chapter goes to Google Translate. Nothing is sent until you turn it on.
- **Browser download.** On desktop, the first time a source needs it, the app downloads the browser it uses for sites with a bot check.
- **Sync.** Sync happens only if you sign in, and only with the server you chose. Your library is locked on your device before it is sent, so the server cannot read it. The server keeps your username, and the name, type, app version and last seen time of your devices.

## Your control

Removing a profile or the app deletes its data from your device. To delete your account on a sync server, ask the person who runs it.

## This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no sign-in, and it loads no fonts or scripts from other companies.

- **The download page** asks GitHub for the newest version of the app when you open it. That request is made from your browser, so GitHub sees your IP address, as with any page that links to a file on GitHub. The answer is kept in your browser for a few minutes, so the page does not ask again. Nothing about it is sent to us.
- **To suggest the right file,** the page reads what kind of device and processor your browser reports. That stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
- **Hosting.** The site is served by Firebase Hosting, which is run by Google. Like any web host, it keeps ordinary server logs of requests, such as the address asked for and your IP address. We do not use them for anything.

## If you contact us

If you write to us on Discord or open an issue on GitHub, we see what you send and your username, and we use it only to answer you. Discord and GitHub have their own privacy rules for what they keep. Anything you post in a GitHub issue is public. See the [contact page](/contact).

## Changes

This page is updated along with the app and the website. If the app or the website starts to send something new, it will be listed here. See also the [terms](/terms).
